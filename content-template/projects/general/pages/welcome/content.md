## What this is

The Lamassu research wiki: notes on PKI, X.509, post-quantum cryptography, RFCs and the rest of what we learn while building Lamassu.

## How to use it

1. Sign in with your GitHub account (you need to be in the research team).
2. Press **Create article**, pick one or more **tags**, write in Markdown and add an edit summary.
3. Every save is a commit in the `research-content` repository: use **View history** to compare or restore revisions.

## Maturity

Mark an article **Draft** while it is work in progress, **Reviewed** once a colleague has read it, and **Validated** when it has been confirmed in practice. Superseded articles become **Deprecated**; nothing is ever deleted.
